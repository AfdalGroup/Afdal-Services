# Afdal Setup Services

Afdal Setup provides a range of consulting and support services for individuals, entrepreneurs, investors, students, skilled workers, and families who are planning to move, invest, start a business, or apply for visas and residency.

Official website:

[https://afdalsetup.com](https://afdalsetup.com)

## Company Registration

Afdal Setup helps clients with company registration and business setup services. This can include guidance on business structures, setup procedures, documentation, and related requirements.

## Immigration Services

Afdal Setup provides immigration consulting for clients who are planning to move abroad for work, study, investment, family, or residency purposes.

Services may include:

- Immigration pathway consultation
- Document preparation guidance
- Case review by experienced professionals
- Support through the application process

## Residency Services

Afdal Setup supports clients who are seeking residency options through business, investment, property purchase, work, study, or other eligible routes.

## Visa Services

Afdal Setup provides support for different visa categories, including:

- Tourist visas
- Work visas
- Study visas
- Residency visas
- Investment-related visas

## Investment Consulting

Afdal Setup provides advisory services for clients interested in international investment opportunities. The team helps clients review suitable options based on their goals, budget, and long-term plans.

## Work Immigration

Afdal Setup supports clients looking for work immigration opportunities by providing guidance and consultation based on available programs and requirements.

## Study Immigration

Afdal Setup provides consultation for students who are planning to study abroad and need support with choosing a pathway, preparing documents, and understanding visa requirements.

## Property Purchase Advisory

Afdal Setup also provides property-related advisory services for clients who are considering property purchase as part of their investment, residency, or relocation plan.

## Support and Consultation
