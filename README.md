# Afdal Group

**Afdal Group** is a Dubai-based business group providing professional services in real estate, investment advisory, residency solutions, company setup, immigration consulting, and international business support.

The group operates through specialized service brands and platforms, helping clients make informed decisions in the UAE and international markets.

## Main Business Areas

Afdal Group covers several key areas, including:

- Real estate brokerage and property investment in Dubai
- Property buying and selling advisory
- Legal review and contract support for real estate transactions
- Residency and visa services
- Company registration and business setup
- Immigration consulting
- Investment advisory
- Work, study, tourist, and residency visa guidance
- Financial and migration consulting

## Afdal Real Estate Brokerage

**Afdal Real Estate Brokerage** provides specialized real estate advisory services in Dubai and the UAE. The company supports clients in buying, selling, investing, and managing property assets.

The real estate team works with a wide network of well-known developers in Dubai and provides guidance throughout the property purchase process.
